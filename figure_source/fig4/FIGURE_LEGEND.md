# Figure 4 legend

**Figure 4 | Cost composition and objective scaling contribute asymmetrically
to finite endpoint responses.** **a,** Median endpoint mean response across 21
independent units on the fixed-regularization `u`–`v` grid. Total scale is `u`
and spatial-cost fraction is `v`; expression and spatial coefficients are
`u(1-v)` and `uv`. The diamond marks the baseline `(u,v)=(1,0.5)`, circles mark
Arm R endpoints and triangles mark Arm N endpoints. A shared colour scale is
used for all methods. **b,** Unit-level 2 × 2 factorial decomposition under
fixed regularization. Translucent points are independent units, thick segments
are interquartile ranges and large points are medians. For each deleted
channel, the joint response equals removal plus compensation plus interaction.
**c,** Pure-expression (`v=0`) and pure-spatial (`v=1`) scale contributions,
calculated within each independent unit as the fixed-regularization response
minus the co-regularized response. Points and error bars show medians and
interquartile ranges; the horizontal zero line denotes no contribution from
relative regularization scale. All applicable regularization terms are scaled
with `u` in the co-regularized control. Balanced OT, UOT and Row-softmax are blue, teal and ochre,
respectively. The panels report descriptive model-internal responses on a
sparse prespecified grid and do not identify a biological causal mechanism.
