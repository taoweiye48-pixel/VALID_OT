**Figure 5 | Held-out-expression utility of finite-response and practical
scores.** **a,** Held-out-expression gain over the frozen fixed-QC
comparator for the finite response at `h=0.01`, its high-accuracy local
reference and the exact endpoint response. Gain is defined as fixed-QC

endpoint response, respectively. **b,** Median internal-fidelity Spearman
correlation with the local reference versus median held-out-expression
NEX-AURC for the eight registered primary practical scores across the 21 main
independent units. Lower NEX-AURC denotes better external selective ranking;
the horizontal dashed line at 1 denotes random ranking. The shared score key
denotes
assigned raw cost (`cost`), barycentric displacement (`bary.`), conditional
entropy (`entropy`), the finite response at `h=0.01` (`s(0.01)`), one minus the
maximum row probability (`low-p`), probability-margin risk (`margin`), source
boundary proximity (`boundary`) and transported-mass deficit (`mass`). Balanced
OT, UOT and Row-softmax are blue, teal and ochre, respectively. These are
descriptive independent-unit summaries. Held-out
expression is a model-conditioned auxiliary witness and does not establish
biological correspondence truth.
